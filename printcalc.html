3D Print Product Pricing Calculator

How to Use
  1. 1Quick Estimate — Filament weight, print time, labor. Fast price with three margin targets.
  2. 2Full Calculator — Add hardware, packaging, and shipping for a full breakdown.
  3. 3Bulk Pricing — Set quantity tiers with per-tier discounts to auto-generate a bulk price table.
  4. 4Rounding — In Settings, choose None, Round Up, Round Down, or Nearest Dollar. Rounding applies to all final prices.
  5. 5Experimental Features — Enable Filament Library and Printer Library in Settings to save and load your own presets.
  6. 6Saved Prints — Hit "Save This Print" to store in browser memory. Export/import as JSON anytime.
💡 Margin math: 60% margin = profit is 60% of sale price. Formula: Price = Cost ÷ (1 − Margin). Rounding applies to the final price, never the cost.
