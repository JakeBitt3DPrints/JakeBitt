One-click effects

Auto-shade adds a soft light-on-top, shadow-underneath gradient to every face. Texture noise gives flat colors that slightly grainy Minecraft look.

Locator bar color

Every Java player has a default color on the locator bar (added in 1.21.6). It comes from your UUID, so it never changes unless someone sets it with /waypoint.

Pixel quality

Going up keeps your skin looking the same and gives you more pixels for detail. Going down merges detail.
64x64Modern format (Java 1.8+ and Bedrock). Separate left and right limbs, outer layer on every part.
64x32Old format from before 1.8. Only a hat layer, and the left limbs copy the right ones. These convert automatically when opened.
Classic4 pixel wide arms (Steve).
