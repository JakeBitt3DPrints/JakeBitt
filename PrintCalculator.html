3D Print Product Pricing Calculator

How to Use
  1. 1Quick Estimate — Fill in filament weight, print time, and labor minutes for a fast price with three margin targets shown.
  2. 2Full Calculator — Add hardware, packaging, and shipping line items. Each row needs a name, quantity, and unit cost.
  3. 3Settings ⚙ — Configure printer wattage, electricity rate, printer cost, labor rate, and waste factor. These apply to all calculations.
  4. 4Custom margin slider — Drag to any margin 1–99%. Above 90% is unusual — use the high-margin warning as a gut check.
  5. 5Saved Prints — Hit "Save Print" to store your current calculation in browser memory. Load or export them any time.
💡 Margin math: 60% margin means profit = 60% of the sale price. Formula: Price = Cost ÷ (1 − Margin). A 90% margin on a $5 print = $50 sale price.
